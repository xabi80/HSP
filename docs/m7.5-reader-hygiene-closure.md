## 3. Empirical findings

### 3.1 Q1 idempotency — marin_semi_trimmed byte-exact zero residuals

The M7.5 audit hypothesised that `HydroDatabase.__post_init__`
symmetrization is idempotent on already-symmetric BEM input
(such as `tests/fixtures/bem/wamit/marin_semi_trimmed.*` from
M5). PR2 confirmed empirically: all four residuals
(`A`, `B`, `A_inf`, `C`) reported `0.000000e+00` on the
marin_semi_trimmed WAMIT fixture — bit-exact zeros, no round-off.
The M6 test suite's byte-diff invariants on marin_semi held
across the PR2 change with no adjustment.

### 3.2 PR1 bit-exact kernel identity

PR1's `asymptote_check_override=None` (the standard path)
must be bit-identical to the pre-PR1 kernel. Verified: max
absolute residual `= 0.0` across 216 036 kernel-array entries
on the marin_semi_trimmed test corpus. The override is a
new code path added alongside the standard path, not a
modification of it.

### 3.3 PR3 tier-2 fixture measurements

`check_hydrostatic_volume` on the terminal-fixture variants
(`rho = 1025 kg/m^3`, body mass `28.67 kg`):

| variant | signed_volume | displaced_mass | residual_fraction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ORIGINAL  | `+3.882e-02 m^3` | `39.79 kg` | `+0.388` |
| CORRECTED | `+3.914e-02 m^3` | `40.12 kg` | `+0.399` |
| full_fix  | `+3.989e-02 m^3` | `40.89 kg` | `+0.426` |

Pairwise `|ΔV| / V(CORRECTED)`:

- ORIG vs CORR: **0.82 %**,
- full_fix vs CORR: **1.92 %**.

Both well below any residual threshold that could catch a
16× `A_inf` error (which was the failure that motivated the
milestone). The tier-2 measurements quantify the tier-1
blind-spot: thin-plate reversals leave a small volumetric
signature. Tier 1 (per-panel ray-parity) covers the blind
spot at panel granularity; tier 2 is the physics-first
backstop against wholly-inverted meshes.

The `+40 %` `residual_fraction` is **reserve buoyancy of the
spar-buoy geometry**, not a mismatch to close. The meshed
waterline displaces ~40.1 kg against a 28.67 kg body — a
documented fixture property, pinned in-suite by
`test_hydrostatic_volume_fixture_measurements_positive`. If
that assertion fails after a mesh or mass change, the tracker
entry `BEM-MESH-STRIP-PANELS-STUDY-FIXTURE` is updated, not
the tolerance.

### 3.5.1 B-asymmetry localization (PR4-A round-2 diagnostic)

B asymmetry localizes to high-omega mesh-resolution noise
(max 6.76e-2 per-omega normalized at `ω = 20.9 rad/s`, isolated
on the surge-pitch off-diagonal (0,4)/(1,3); the raw
`max|B − B.T| = 0.125` at `ω = 19.45 rad/s` is the single
outlier that dominates the total-omega metadata residual). In
the physics-relevant band `ω ≤ 10 rad/s` — which sits well
above `ω_n,heave ≈ 1.85 rad/s` for `T_n ≈ 3.4 s` — median
per-omega normalized B asymmetry drops to ~4e-4 and A stays
flat at ~1.5e-4. Test 2's physics-band assertions are set on
this restricted range (`max|B − B.T|_{ω ≤ 10} ≈ 2.06e-2`);
the total-omega metadata residuals remain pinned separately as
fixture properties. Same Item 25 small-body /
high-frequency-mesh-resolution split that motivated PR1's
override applies — the BEM is trustworthy in its physics band
but the asymptote regime is a separate class of concern.

## 4. The Q2 record — six amendments + one test-driven correction

Q2 (panel-normal validation utility) is the section of this
closure that matters most as institutional memory. The
algorithm underwent six delete-and-replace amendments across
PR3 pre-flight before landing at per-panel ray-parity. Told
honestly:

1. **Reader-insertion-point discovery (audit).** The M7.5
   pre-milestone reader audit (`docs/audits/m7.5-reader-audit.md`)
   found that BEM-reader integration would require touching
   three separate readers plus their tests. Q2 lock chose
   the standalone-utility path: `floatsim/hydro/mesh_hygiene.py`
   invoked by callers, not wired into readers. This decision
   held.

2. **Centroid-outward test (original Q2 lock, `f08e1ff`).**
   For each panel, dot the panel normal with the
   `centroid − mesh.mean()` vector; positive = outward. The
   algorithm inverted on concave features like the plate top
   annulus of the terminal fixture — where the arithmetic
   mesh centroid happens to lie in the empty volume beside
   the concavity. Detected by the very first fixture-based
   assertion.

3. **Edge-consistency + signed volume for closed manifolds
   (first amendment, `4700106`).** Two-pass: (a) verify each
   edge is shared by exactly two panels in opposite
   directions; (b) verify total signed volume is positive.
   Correct on synthetic closed geometry (verified with a
   discriminator test), but the terminal fixture is NOT a
   closed manifold — 96 open edges at the plate-spar junction.
   Failed at the manifold check.

4. **Per-component + ray-parity for open components (second
   amendment).** Flood-fill parity propagation for each
   connected component; signed-volume orientation for closed
   components; ray-parity majority-vote against the whole
   mesh for open components. Correct in general, but the
   terminal fixture's thin plate has strip panels that are
   traversal-inconsistent with faces even in the correct
   state — so within-plate flood-fill mis-classified 24
   strips relative to 192 faces.

5. **The Step 0 diagnostic gate (Q2 sixth amendment
   discussion).** Instead of iterating on more algorithm
   variants, we measured. Per-panel ray-parity on both
   fixtures showed:
   - ORIGINAL: 216 inward (192 horizontal + 24 strip)
   - CORRECTED: 24 inward (0 horizontal + 24 strip)

   The 192 target the red gate had been calibrated against
   for five rounds was **the footprint of the study's z-band
   heuristic**, not a measurement of what was misoriented. The
   study's heuristic was a partial fix; the "corrected"
   fixture still carried 24 misoriented panels that the
   heuristic never touched, invisibly to Capytaine's `A_inf`
   result.

6. **Per-panel ray-parity as the final algorithm (Q2 sixth
   and final amendment, `e6e3400`).** Flood-fill parity
   propagation and signed-volume orientation deleted despite
   verified correctness on synthetic closed geometry —
   they answered a different question (relative orientation
   within a shell) than the one that mattered (per-panel
   correctness against the mesh's topology). Ray-parity is
   assumption-free (no manifold, no components, no parity
   classes) and matches the measured ground truth of 216 /
   24 exactly. Load-bearing protection is tier 2
   (`check_hydrostatic_volume`), which sees reversed voluminous
   features via the divergence-theorem signature; tier 1
   catches the tier-2 thin-plate blind-spot.

7. **The seventh, test-driven correction (open-boundary
   warning).** Pre-flight pass 1 after the sixth amendment
   surfaced two failing tests on open synthetic shells
   (topless cube; isolated plate beside a cube) — expected
   ray-parity to detect a flipped panel that the algorithm
   topologically cannot detect (the ray exits through the
   opening → 0 crossings → parity even → reported outward).
   Rather than delete the tests, we added the open-boundary
   `UserWarning` to the reader path
   (`n_open_edges > 0` → warn), rewrote the tests as
   documented-false-negative tests, and pinned the terminal
   fixture's 96-edge open-boundary property so the warning
   fires in-suite on every validate/fix call.

### Root cause named

Q2 was locked as a topology problem. It is a physics-screening
problem. The topology framing produced five rounds of amendment
that each answered a slightly different topology question
(closed manifold; multi-shell polygon soup; per-component
open shell) — all correct within their assumptions, none
protective against the failure class that motivated the
milestone.

Compounding it: the red gate was calibrated against the study
heuristic's output (192 flipped panels) for five rounds
instead of against the measurement. The heuristic was
convenient (already existed, produced a number) but not
authoritative. The Step 0 diagnostic gate — measure first,
design against the measurement — was the correction mechanism
that broke the amendment cycle.

### Correction mechanism named

**Measure before designing.** Steps 0 diagnostics on both
terminal fixtures established the objective ground truth
(216 / 24) before any tier-1 algorithm change was proposed.
That single number invalidated 5 rounds of prior algorithm
work in ~15 minutes.

**Physics-first protective screen alongside topological
detection.** Tier 2 `check_hydrostatic_volume` is the failure-
mode-aligned metric — a reversed voluminous face flips the
signed volume, independent of any topology assumption. Tier 1
per-panel ray-parity fills the thin-plate blind spot at panel
granularity. Neither tier alone is sufficient; both together
are.

## 7. What M7.5 hands forward

M7.5 delivers the tooling for the spar-fin study resumption
(`scratch-spar-fin-decay`) to pick up its terminal-gate work
from a hardened codebase. The resumption checklist:

1. **Rebase `scratch-spar-fin-decay` onto post-close `main`.**
   The three hardened readers (Q1 HydroDatabase, Q2
   mesh_hygiene, Q3 kernel override) are now in `floatsim/`;
   the study should absorb them via a straightforward
   rebase.
2. **Retire `capytaine_run.py`'s `A_sym` workaround.** The
   study's script currently pre-symmetrizes A/B before
   passing to FloatSim. That block can be deleted;
   `HydroDatabase.__post_init__` handles it and records the
   residual on metadata.
3. **Adopt workflow alpha via `floatsim.hydro.mesh_hygiene`.**
   Replace `studies/spar-fin-decay/fix_mesh_normals.py` with
   `validate_panel_normals(mesh)` + `fix_panel_normals(mesh)`
   at the top of `capytaine_run.py`. The auto-fix path
   catches all 216 misoriented panels (192 horizontal + 24
   strip) — a strict superset of what
   `fix_mesh_normals.py` fixed.
4. **STEP-A-FINDING addendum.** Add a subsection recording
   the three documented fixture properties (see §3.4): 24
   misoriented strips, +40 % reserve-buoyancy residual, 96
   open edges. Cite the tier-2 measurements and the
   corresponding in-suite pins. These are documented
   properties, not bugs — future study steps should not
   open them as findings.
5. **Steps C-G proceed with the +40 % residual feeding
   Step D's equilibrium expectations.** The reserve buoyancy
   of the spar-buoy geometry means static equilibrium sees
   `40.1 kg` displaced against `28.67 kg` body mass —
   Step D's static solver needs to expect this residual
   rather than treat it as a solver-convergence problem. This
   is Scenario B territory (moderate-rotation multi-body BEM
   at wave-tank scale); the fixture-property quantification
   is provided in advance so Step D can be scoped
   accordingly.

M7.5 does not perform any of these steps itself — they are
