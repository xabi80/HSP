# Questions for OSU HWRL (Large Wave Flume) — station-keeping mooring

Draft for Xabier to send. Context: the flume mooring for the TEAMER tests is an X-spread of four
soft, horizontal lines at the still-water line (SWL), running to anchors about 5 m up- and
downstream of the article, near the side walls (see `README.md` / `mooring_layout.png`). The
articles are a single buoy (~22 kg), a 4-buoy cluster (~88 kg) and a 4×4 platform (~358 kg,
2.06 m wide at the 45° test orientation). Mean line tensions are a few N to ~40 N. The largest
line tension is ≤ ~70 N for the platform, and that is a conservative bound.

## Anchoring (this decides the design geometry)

1. Are there anchor points on the flume walls at or near the SWL (embedded inserts, rails, bolt
   patterns), about 5 m up- and downstream of the planned test section? If not, can we use a
   beam or frame across the flume, or the instrument carriage, as an anchor at SWL height?
2. If only floor anchors are possible: what is the anchor spacing, and can lines run ≥ 10 m from
   the article? Short lines to the floor in 2.7 m of water would be steep and would couple into
   heave.
3. What is the working-load limit for the anchor points (we expect ≤ 100 N per line)?

## Hardware and instrumentation

4. Does HWRL have a standard soft-mooring kit (extension springs or elastic cord) with about
   2 m of linear stroke at 2–35 N/m per line? Or is there typical practice for floating models
   of this size?
5. Are in-line load cells (0–100 N range) available for four lines, mounted at the anchors?
6. Can we do a static pull test in the flume (winch or force gauge plus position tracking) to
   verify mooring stiffness and pretension before testing?

## Test-section logistics

7. What are the planned water depth (we assume 2.7 m) and the test-section position relative to
   the wavemaker and the beach? Where are the wave gauges?
8. Does the motion-tracking coverage allow for a mean downstream offset of up to ~0.5–0.8 m plus
   the wave-frequency motion?
9. Are there constraints on lines running along the walls (other instruments, carriage rails,
   access for rigging the bridles at the spar waterline)?
