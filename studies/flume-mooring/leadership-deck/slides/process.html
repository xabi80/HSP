Process

How we designed it

1

Agree the pass/fail criteria first. Seven criteria, set before any design work: position, lean, rocking period, slack, loads.

2

Simulate every option in FloatSim. Attachment height, cord stiffness and tension, for all three articles.

3

Choose by a written rule. The rule picks the design, not case-by-case judgement.

4

Verify the choice. Full FloatSim wave runs of the chosen design, including the worst waves.

5

Record every decision. Specification, design basis and figures are all generated from the same simulation files.

House rule: every number is recomputed from the record, never copied, and any disagreement is written down.

Flume mooring design · leadership briefing

5

The method matters as much as the answer. We fixed the acceptance criteria before designing, so nobody could move the goalposts. Every option was simulated in FloatSim rather than estimated by hand. A written decision rule chose the design. The chosen design was then run through full wave simulations. And the specification tables and figures are generated from the same simulation files, so the documents cannot disagree with the analysis.
