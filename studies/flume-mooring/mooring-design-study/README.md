# Mooring design study — technical deck

A 27-slide deck for a technical audience. It covers the same concepts as the leadership briefing
(`../leadership-deck/`) at engineering depth:
- the criteria and the ζ/3 rule, and the method;
- the revisions, and why the pin plane failed (k·h²);
- the attachment-height sweep;
- the three configurations (single buoy, cluster, 4×4 platform), each with its plan and elevation to scale;
- the verification runs, including the moored-vs-free comparison and the buoy's stability battery;
- the extreme cord set: the k scan, the declared effects, the at-rest-matched pretension and the pull identification;
- loads, hardware, the pretension evaluation, creep, the procedure, compliance, limitations and next steps.

It is built from MOORING-SPEC rev C.1.

## Items the deck states that the spec does not

These come from the committed records and are itemised here, as the standing rule requires:

- **Moored vs free, slide 13** (`viewer_rows_revc/`, 48 runs, free runs unrestrained):
  - The heave RAO changes by up to +9.0 % (buoy) and +7.8 % (cluster) at T = 2.2 s, H = 0.12 m.
    That is outside criterion 6's ±3 %.
  - 2.2 s is inside the flume's sloshing exclusion window (2.08–2.30 s), so it is not a test
    period.
  - The platform's tilt resonance at H = 0.04 m gives +4.6 %.
  - The formal criterion-6 check, with the 60 / 120 s numerical restraint, is Phase D.
- **Compliance, slide 24:**
  - The mean tilt under waves (criterion 1, ≤ 3° at the maximum H) and the heave-RAO form of
    criterion 6 are marked "Phase D".
  - The extreme set's lines, uncovered in the H = 0.5 m troughs, are marked "reported". The
    record reports that exposure without a verdict, and line drag is not modelled.
