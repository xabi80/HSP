Three articles are tested in turn in the OSU Large Wave Flume at 1:50. The table gives the unmoored heave and tilt natural periods from FloatSim's modal analysis: heave 2.56 to 2.61 s, tilt 2.76 to 2.92 s. Both sit inside the wave band, so the response tests excite them directly, and the mooring must not move them. The flume BEM databases use a 12-sided waterline, which makes all these resonances about 0.04 to 0.08 s long; the regeneration at 36 sides is pending, and the interference test is relative to the unmoored article on the same database. The matrix has an operational band for the motions and an extreme band for loads and offsets.
