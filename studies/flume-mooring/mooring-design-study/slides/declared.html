Two things about the extreme set. Left: it changes the article's response, and that is declared rather than treated as a failed criterion. Its tilt shift is -3.8 and -4.7 percent, and its surge period drops to 10.5 and 9.6 s, which breaks the 14 s separation criterion and amplifies the wave-frequency surge by 13 and 15 percent at 3.5 s, from 1/(1 - (T/T_surge)^2). Criteria 2 and 4 cannot both hold at H = 0.5 m; the tracking window governs, because these tests measure loads and survival, and the tank and FloatSim see the same mooring. This was accepted on 2026-09-25. Right: the pretension. The code's nominal T0 is the tension at the untilted chord; the calm tilt shortens each chord by about 15 mm, so a stiff cord at the same nominal T0 would sit much slacker at rest, 1.21 and 0.87 N instead of 1.44 and 1.42, and settle at a different tilt. Matching the at-rest tension makes the calm line forces identical, so the operational settle is also the extreme set's equilibrium, as the static residuals confirm.
