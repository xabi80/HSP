Method

Criteria first, every option simulated in FloatSim

1

Criteria agreed before design. Seven pass/fail criteria, confirmed in Phase B.

2

FloatSim for every option. Moored decks, static settle, modal analysis, rigid pulls, and regular-wave runs with wave-relative Morison drag and the drift sum applied in-run.

3

Selection by a written rule. Hard criteria, then the most pretension, the attachment closest to the waterline, the stiffest surge.

4

Verification runs. The chosen designs in FloatSim wave runs, operational band and H = 0.5 m.

5

Generated documents. The specification's tables and figures are built from the same JSON records.

Drift sum: the recorded fixed-body bound (splash-zone drag + Havelock term) plus FloatSim's own mean force, so conservative. LEVEL1 kinematics are valid to 5.7°: resonant results are indicative.

Mooring design study · MOORING-SPEC rev C.1

5

The method. The criteria were fixed first, so the design could not move the goalposts. Every option was a FloatSim deck, evaluated the same way: a static settle for the calm tilt and at-rest tensions, a modal analysis for the natural periods, rigid pulls for the stiffness, and regular-wave runs. The drift force is deliberately conservative: the recorded fixed-body bound is applied in-run at each spar's waterline, on top of FloatSim's own mean force. A written rule picked the design, the chosen designs were verified in wave runs, and the specification is generated from the same records. FloatSim runs at LEVEL1, valid to 0.1 rad; near resonance the articles tilt 10 to 30 degrees, so those predictions are indicative.
