The 4 by 4 platform, to scale. Four two-leg bridles, eight legs, run from the up- and down-stream row spars to the wall anchors, two legs per anchor. The platform is the test matrix's 2.4 m square: buoy centres on a 0.8 m grid, 2.69 m wide with the heave plates at 45 degrees, so it keeps only 0.49 m from each wall, below the 0.6 m clearance criterion. The coupled sidewall study shows the walls change its heave response by at most 3.3 percent, no more than at the earlier 0.80 m; accepting the clearance on that evidence, or reducing the platform, is the open decision. The legs attach 0.15 m below still water. The operational legs are 8.36 N/m; the extreme legs five and a half times stiffer, at the same 1.44 N at rest. The heave natural period moves 0.04 percent; the tilt period 0.74 percent against a 1.01 percent limit. The platform's up-flume anchors carry the largest working load in the study, 246.9 N, inside the 300 N anchor rating.
