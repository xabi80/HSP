Every number in this deck can be traced to a record in the study folder. The specification and the figures are generated from these JSON files by build_spec.py and mooring_figures.py, and the design basis records each decision, the evidence behind it and the disagreements that were settled. The moored-versus-free comparison comes from the 48 runs behind the 3D motion viewer.
