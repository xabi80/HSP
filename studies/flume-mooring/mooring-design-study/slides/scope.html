Three articles are moored in turn in the OSU Large Wave Flume. The single buoy is one spar with a heave plate; the cluster is four buoys hinged to a hub at 45 degrees; the platform is sixteen buoys in a 4 by 4 array, 2.06 m wide at 45 degrees. The unmoored periods are FloatSim modal values; the tilt resonances sit between 2.76 and 2.92 s, inside the wave band. The matrix has two bands. The operational band, up to H = 0.12 m, is where the motions are measured to validate FloatSim, so the mooring must not disturb them. The extreme band, H = 0.2 to 0.5 m, is for loads, offsets and survival. The steepness cap H/lambda of about 0.08 limits which periods take the larger heights: H = 0.5 m only from T = 2.01 s. Every period stays at least 5 percent clear of the flume's transverse sloshing cut-ons.
