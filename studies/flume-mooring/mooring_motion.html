Generated by mooring_figures.py from the spec's own tables.

Data (rev C): each case is the article's calm equilibrium with one cord set, from FloatSim — the moored settle of the cluster and platform (moored_equilibrium.json) and the buoy's checked static solve — shown as displacements from the unmoored (deck) equilibrium. Anchors, attachment points, pretension and at-rest line tensions are MOORING-SPEC rev C's own numbers (spec_statics.json, spec_statics_extreme.json); the platform's bridles are two lines per anchor. The calm tilt of the cluster and platform (≈ 1°, the confirmed limit) is the pretension moment about the pins. Not animated: the earlier animated cases used a superseded mooring (lines at the still-water line) and are withdrawn; no rev C wave run has saved frames. The wave-case predictions (offsets, line loads, tilt) are tabled in MOORING-SPEC rev C. Validity: FloatSim integrates rotations with small-angle kinematics (full rotation kinematics, "LEVEL2", are not yet wired). Tilts above 0.1 rad (5.7°) are outside that range and are flagged ⚠ as indicative only; every near-resonance and extreme prediction in the spec is beyond it.
