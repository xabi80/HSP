Mooring layout — plan and side view, to scale

X-spread of four soft lines (linear spring + low-stretch rope) from wall anchors 5 m up- and downstream, attached at the still-water line: the buoy's spar collar, the cluster's four spars (one line each), and the platform's upstream and downstream rows of four spars through two-leg bridles.

Data: FloatSim time domain — Cummins radiation + velocity-level KKT joints + Morison drag on spar and heave plate relative to the wave velocity, with the design mooring as linear springs at the attachments. Each case is the settled response fitted with harmonics 1–3 of the wave frequency, so it loops over one period. Line tension = pretension + line stiffness × stretch. Not shown: the mean drift offset (the time-domain model has no splash-zone drift; the readout quotes its upper bound). Rigid skeleton per the joint topology: each buoy's spar hangs from its pin and tilts with the buoy. Tilts above ~10° are beyond the model's small-angle range and are indicative.
